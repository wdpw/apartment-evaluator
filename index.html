Default Scoring Rules

Price: $1,800 or less = best; $1,801–1,900 = very good; $1,901–2,000 = slight penalty; $2,001–2,100 = larger penalty; over $2,100 = low score.
Commute: 10 min or less = best; 11–15 min = very good; 16–20 min = average; 21–30 min = penalty; over 30 min = low score.
Building Age: 2020 or newer = best; 2015–2019 = good; 2010–2014 = average; 2000–2009 = low; before 2000 = lower.
Den: Good-sized space > small space > none. Laundry: In-suite > shared > none. Parking: Included indoor > paid indoor > none.
